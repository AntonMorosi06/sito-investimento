# MicroBot Labs — Business and Education Direction

MicroBot Labs is currently a technical project in pre-startup phase. It should not be treated as a finished company yet.

The first strategic direction is educational robotics. MicroBot Labs can become a platform for explaining modular robotics, embedded systems, telemetry, swarm behavior, dashboards, and simulation through accessible prototypes and visual tools.

The second strategic direction is technical prototyping. The project can support the creation of dashboards, ESP32 prototypes, simulation tools, 3D visualization, documentation, and experimental interfaces.

The third strategic direction is research-oriented experimentation. MicroBot Labs can become a structured environment for studying distributed robotic behavior, node communication, physical and virtual telemetry, and human-machine interaction.

The fourth strategic direction is portfolio and professional positioning. Even before becoming a company, MicroBot Labs can demonstrate advanced skills in software engineering, embedded systems, robotics, simulation, documentation, cybersecurity-oriented design, and technical communication.

The project should validate interest before choosing a final business model.

The key validation questions are:

- Do students or makers understand the project quickly?
- Would an educational lab use a simplified version of it?
- Is the dashboard useful as a teaching tool?
- Is the prototype reproducible?
- Which part creates the most interest: hardware, simulation, dashboard, documentation, or 3D visualization?
- Would someone pay for a kit, a course, a dashboard, a prototype service, or a technical demonstration?

The company should be created only after the project becomes understandable, demonstrable, and externally useful.
