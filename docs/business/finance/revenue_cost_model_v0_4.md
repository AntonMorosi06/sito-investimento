# MicroBot Labs — Revenue and Cost Model v0.4

Status: financial hypothesis  
Created: 2026-05-18  
Hardware validation status: not hardware-validated  
Revenue status: no revenue validated

## 1. Purpose

This document defines possible revenue and cost models for MicroBot Labs.

At v0.4, revenue is hypothetical. The project should not assume sales before technical validation and external feedback.

## 2. Cost model

The immediate cost is the v0.4 minimum hardware kit.

| Cost category | Estimated range | Timing |
|---|---:|---|
| v0.4 minimum ESP32 kit | 60-130 EUR | immediate |
| Domain | 10-30 EUR/year | optional |
| Hosting | 0-15 EUR/month | optional |
| Demo assets | 0-50 EUR | optional |
| 3D printing | later | after physical design |
| Drone hardware | later | after ESP32/node validation |
| Company/legal costs | later | only after clearer traction |

## 3. Possible revenue streams

| Revenue stream | Timing | Risk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free open-source repo + sponsorship | early | low-medium | Good for credibility, weak revenue |
| Paid workshop | after first demo | medium | Best first revenue experiment |
| Educational kit | after reproducibility | medium-high | Requires hardware validation and packaging |
| Course material | after stable docs | medium | Could be PDF/video/workbook |
| Consulting/demo setup | after strong demo | medium | Small custom support |
| University collaboration | after professor review | medium | May not be direct revenue |
| Full product | much later | high | Requires validation, safety, support and manufacturing |

## 4. Best first revenue experiment

The best first revenue experiment is not a physical product.

The best first experiment is:

A guided MicroBot Labs workshop or tutorial package where participants learn ESP32 serial validation, dashboard telemetry and first LED state node.

This has lower risk because it sells knowledge and guidance, not an unvalidated hardware product.

## 5. Pricing hypotheses

Possible early prices:

| Offer | Hypothetical price | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Free GitHub repository | 0 EUR | credibility and distribution |
| Guided 1:1 setup/help session | 20-50 EUR | only after the demo works |
| Small group workshop | 10-30 EUR/person | educational validation |
| Digital workbook | 5-15 EUR | after docs are polished |
| Hardware kit | cost + margin | only after reproducible hardware |

These prices are not validated.

## 6. Financial recommendation

Do not spend like a startup before validating like a maker.

For the next stage, keep costs under 130 EUR and focus on producing evidence.
