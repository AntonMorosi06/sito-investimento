## 1. Purpose

This document records the external business frameworks used to structure the MicroBot Labs business analysis.

## 2. Business Model Canvas

The Business Model Canvas is used because it organizes a business model into nine core blocks covering customers, value, channels, relationships, revenue, resources, activities, partnerships and costs.

External sources consulted for framework alignment:

- Strategyzer — Business Model Canvas and nine building-block logic.
- OpenLearn / Open University — Business Model Canvas as nine building blocks across offer, customers, infrastructure and financial viability.
- Atlassian — practical explanation of the BMC as a strategic mapping tool.

## 3. Lean Canvas

Lean Canvas logic is used to stress-test the startup hypothesis around problem, solution, customer segment, unique value proposition, channels, revenue, costs, metrics and unfair advantage.

## 4. Application to MicroBot Labs

These frameworks are not treated as proof that MicroBot Labs is already a company.

They are used as structured thinking tools to separate:

- what exists;
- what is hypothesized;
- what must be validated;
- what should not be claimed yet.
